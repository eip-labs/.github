**EIP-Labs** is the Research and Development (R&D) laboratory of [**EIP-Technologies**](https://eip-technologies.com). 

Our mission is to **research, develop, and architect** innovative methods, foundational systems, and developer tools for the next generation of advanced artificial intelligence, with a commitment to **Performance, Security, and Flexibility**.

---

### 🌐 What We Publish Here

This GitHub organization is our public space to share selected work with the community:
* **Research & Publications:** Whitepapers and technical insights on autonomous agent architectures.
* **Open Projects & Tools:** Production-ready runtime components, libraries, and utilities.
* **Benchmarks:** Reproducible performance tests and experimental prototypes.
